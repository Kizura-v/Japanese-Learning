# Daily Japanese Training — E-R-K

**Goal:** Build natural conversational Japanese through sentence construction, particles, transformations, and speaking drills. Study **15 minutes a day**.

## E-R-K notation

- **E — English:** natural intended meaning, not a literal word-by-word gloss.
- **R — Romanji:** consistent Hepburn romanization with macrons.
- **K — Kana:** complete phonetic Japanese reading, primarily **hiragana**, retaining katakana for loanwords. Japanese does **not** normally write entire native sentences in katakana. The main Google Sheet stores this reading in its **Hiragana** column.
- **J — Japanese reference:** natural mixed kanji, hiragana, and katakana. Use it for checking, not as the first cue.

The training display is **E | R | K | J**; cover the R/K/J columns while recalling the answer. The master vocabulary dataset keeps the original eight-column spreadsheet schema.

## Daily routine (15 minutes)

1. **2 min — Recall:** say yesterday's three sentences without reading the Japanese.
2. **4 min — Build:** assemble a sentence from topic, time, location, object and final verb. Do not force every slot.
3. **4 min — Transform:** change the sentence into a question, negative, past, polite, or request.
4. **3 min — Speak:** use a realistic family, shopping, greenhouse, or work scenario. Say both sides aloud.
5. **2 min — Review:** mark phrases as Easy / Hesitant / Missed; repeat missed items tomorrow.

**Core ordering:** [topic は] [time] [place で / destination に] [object を] **predicate**. Japanese is flexible, but the predicate normally comes last. Omit an understood subject rather than repeatedly saying 私 or あなた.

## Day 01 — Sentence order

**Pattern:** [time] は [place] で [object] を [verb].

| E | R | K | J |
|---|---|---|---|
| Today I'll eat at home. | Kyō wa ie de taberu. | きょうはいえでたべる。 | 今日は家で食べる。 |
| Tomorrow I'll buy vegetables. | Ashita wa yasai o kau. | あしたはやさいをかう。 | 明日は野菜を買う。 |
| The baby is sleeping. | Akachan ga neteru. | あかちゃんがねてる。 | 赤ちゃんが寝てる。 |
| I'll work in the greenhouse. | Hausu de sagyō suru. | ハウスでさぎょうする。 | ハウスで作業する。 |

**Build:** swap 今日は → 明日は, 家で → ハウスで, 食べる → 作業する. Do not insert を when the verb has no direct object.

## Day 02 — Choose the particle

- **は (wa):** what the sentence is about; also contrast.
- **が (ga):** subject/focus, especially identifying who or what.
- **を (o):** direct object; sometimes a traversed route.
- **に (ni):** destination, target, time point, or location of existence.
- **で (de):** location of an action or means/tool.
- **へ (e):** direction of movement.

| E | R | K | J |
|---|---|---|---|
| I'm going to the greenhouse. | Hausu ni iku. | ハウスにいく。 | ハウスに行く。 |
| I'm working in the greenhouse. | Hausu de hataraiteru. | ハウスではたらいてる。 | ハウスで働いてる。 |
| The hammer is over there. | Hanmā wa asoko ni aru. | ハンマーはあそこにある。 | ハンマーはあそこにある。 |
| I'll pull the film. | Firumu o hipparu. | フィルムをひっぱる。 | フィルムを引っ張る。 |

**Drill:** explain why に, で, に, and を are different in the four examples.

## Day 03 — Statements into questions

In casual Japanese, rising intonation can make a question; **か** is not required.

| E | R | K | J |
|---|---|---|---|
| Are you coming? | Kuru? | くる？ | 来る？ |
| Where are you going? | Doko iku? | どこいく？ | どこ行く？ |
| What should I do? | Nani sureba ii? | なにすればいい？ | 何すればいい？ |
| Is this okay? | Kore de ii? | これでいい？ | これでいい？ |

**Drill:** answer each question in a full sentence, then in a natural short response.

## Day 04 — Transform one verb

| E | R | K | J |
|---|---|---|---|
| I eat / I'll eat. | Taberu. | たべる。 | 食べる。 |
| I don't eat / won't eat. | Tabenai. | たべない。 | 食べない。 |
| I ate. | Tabeta. | たべた。 | 食べた。 |
| I didn't eat. | Tabenakatta. | たべなかった。 | 食べなかった。 |
| I'm eating. | Tabeteru. | たべてる。 | 食べてる。 |
| I'll eat (polite). | Tabemasu. | たべます。 | 食べます。 |

**Drill:** repeat with 行く (iku), then する (suru). Watch for 行った / 行って.

## Day 05 — Greenhouse teamwork

| E | R | K | J |
|---|---|---|---|
| Want me to come up? | Ue ni ikō ka? | うえにいこうか？ | 上に行こうか？ |
| Should I go down? | Shita ni orita hō ga ii? | したにおりたほうがいい？ | 下に降りたほうがいい？ |
| Need a hand? | Tetsudaō ka? | てつだおうか？ | 手伝おうか？ |
| Hold it there. | Soko osaetete. | そこおさえてて。 | そこ押さえてて。 |
| Pull a little more. | Mō sukoshi hippatte. | もうすこしひっぱって。 | もう少し引っ張って。 |

**Contrast:** ～ようか？ offers an action; ～たほうがいい？ asks whether an action is advisable.

## Common casual reductions

| Full form | Natural casual | Function |
|---|---|---|
| ～ている | ～てる | Ongoing action / resulting state |
| ～ておく | ～とく | Do something in advance |
| ～てしまう | ～ちゃう | Completion, sometimes regret |
| ～なければならない | ～なきゃ | Have to / must |
| ～てはだめ | ～ちゃだめ | Don't / not allowed |
| ～のだ | ～んだ | Explanation / context |

## Quality rules

- Prefer **casual standard Japanese** for family and farm work; add polite alternatives when speaking with strangers or officials.
- Mark Kansai-ben and Tosa-ben explicitly rather than presenting dialect as standard Japanese.
- Distinguish natural meaning from literal grammar and explain why each particle is used.
- Do not treat all Japanese nouns as requiring a topic or subject marker.
- Keep the Google Sheets vocabulary format exactly: Eng | Romanji | Hiragana | Japanese | Examples | Group | Usage | Register.
- The [Google Sheets workbook](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1d9_zZAU9MBIifsuHmX5HsnhGb4KQBnjrIFp7xTV9KDk/edit) remains the everyday study interface; the repository holds a versioned snapshot and this training guide.
